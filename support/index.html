Reporting a problem

Include the chapter and level, your iPhone model, your iOS and game versions, and what happened. If you can reproduce the issue, include the steps.

Reporting an inappropriate ad

If you see an inappropriate or age-inappropriate ad in Shiverglass, email us to report the ad. Include the advertiser or product name if visible, what concerned you, and roughly when the ad appeared. A screenshot is optional. Please remove personal information before attaching it.
