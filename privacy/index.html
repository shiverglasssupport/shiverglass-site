Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

This policy covers the SHIVERGLASS iPhone game, its support website and support emails. The developer of SHIVERGLASS is responsible for the game’s local data handling and for responding to support and privacy requests. Contact shiverglass.support@gmail.com.

1. Game progress and settings

The game saves your completed levels, stars, records and solve times, unfinished boards, remaining attempts, undo history, hints, and language and audio preferences in the app’s storage on your device. It uses this data to restore your game and settings. No account is required, and this information is not sent to a SHIVERGLASS server.

Your local data remains until the app’s data is deleted. On iPhone, use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → SHIVERGLASS → Delete App to remove the installed app and its local data. Offloading preserves app data. Any copies in device backups are managed through Apple’s backup settings.

2. Optional rewarded ads and privacy choices

Advertising provider: Shiverglass offers optional rewarded ads in builds where advertising is enabled. The ads are provided by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK for iOS. Watching an ad can unlock one exact strike hint. Standard hints and gameplay remain available without watching an ad. Test builds may use Google’s demo ads.

Data collected by Google: When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information automatically from the app and device. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) may also exchange technical information with Google to check whether a consent prompt is needed and to record advertising privacy choices.

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address.
  • Device or app identifiers, which may include the iOS advertising identifier where available and permitted.
  • Advertising and interaction data, such as ads displayed, views and taps.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and app performance information.

Google can use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent invalid activity, and operate and improve its services. Google may share information with advertising partners as described in its policies. See Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

When Google makes an advertising privacy form available for your region, open Settings → Privacy and Support → Ad privacy choices to review or change your choices. Use that form to decline consent when offered or withdraw it for future processing. You can also manage permissions for cross-app tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Apple’s tracking control and Google’s advertising privacy choices are separate settings. The game uses the SDK’s reward notification to unlock and save the hint for your current board. Google retains information according to its data retention policy. To request deletion of information held by Google, use the privacy request resources below.

3. Support and website visits

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your device model, game version or a screenshot. We use it to answer your message, investigate the issue and follow up as needed. Gmail delivers and stores our support email. We retain correspondence as needed to answer your request and follow up, or longer if required to meet legal obligations or handle a dispute. To request deletion of correspondence, contact us as described below.

The site has no account, contact form or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details when serving the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub handles and retains website logs under its own privacy policy.

4. Privacy requests

Write to shiverglass.support@gmail.com to ask about data held by the developer or to request access, correction or deletion. If your request concerns a support thread, please write from the address you used to contact us. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify the request. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, request a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. If your request concerns data held by Google, you can also use the privacy options and request channels provided by Google.

5. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change requires fresh consent, we will request it as applicable.
